How Jr's Tacos Went from Social Media Only to a Fully Digital Customer Experience

Jr's Tacos had built a loyal local following, but customers depended on social media posts to find truck locations, menu items, hours, and ordering information. Altared Alchemie transformed their digital presence by creating a centralized customer experience designed to increase visibility, simplify ordering, and support long-term business growth.

The Challenge

Jr's Tacos had no dedicated website and relied primarily on Facebook and social media updates to communicate with customers.

Important business information was spread across multiple platforms, making it difficult for customers to:

  • • Find truck locations
  • • View the full menu
  • • Place online orders
  • • Request catering services
  • • Leave reviews
  • • Learn about the brand

Without a centralized digital presence, valuable customer opportunities were being lost.

The Solution

Altared Alchemie designed and launched a complete digital platform focused on customer convenience, discoverability, and business growth.

  • • Custom mobile responsive website
  • • Full Spanish language experience
  • • Truck location tracking system
  • • Facebook schedule integration
  • • StreetFoodFinder backup integration
  • • Complete digital menu with photos and pricing
  • • Direct Clover ordering integration
  • • Popular menu item highlights
  • • Interactive order planning tray
  • • Catering inquiry system
  • • Google review integration
  • • Professional food photography
  • • Social media content creation
  • • Search engine optimization
  • • Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok connections

The Results

A Centralized Digital Home

Customers now have one destination for everything Jr's Tacos offers.

Improved Ordering Experience

Every menu item connects directly to Clover, reducing friction between discovery and purchase.

Expanded Audience Reach

Bilingual functionality allows the business to better serve both English and Spanish-speaking customers.

Professional Brand Presence

Custom food photography and a modern website elevate the brand's credibility and customer trust.

Future Growth Infrastructure

The website provides a scalable foundation for promotions, catering opportunities, SEO growth, and future marketing campaigns.

Jr's Tacos now has more than a website.

They have a complete digital storefront that helps customers discover the brand, find the truck, explore the menu, place orders, book catering, and engage with the business from any device.

This project demonstrates how Altared Alchemie helps local businesses turn fragmented information into scalable digital experiences that drive growth.
