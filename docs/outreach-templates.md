# Outreach Templates — First $1,000

## Strategy
Your fastest path to $1,000 is the $500 AI Readiness Call.
Two sales = $1,000. Target: warm contacts first, then LinkedIn.

---

## Template A — Warm DM / Text (Friends, Former Clients, Network)
*Best for: people who know you and your work*

```
Hey [Name] — hope you're doing well.

I just launched Altared Alchemie, my AI consulting practice, and I'm 
doing a limited number of AI Readiness Calls this month.

It's a 60-min strategy session where I map out exactly where AI can 
save your business time and money — plus a written report with 3 
prioritized recommendations, delivered within 48 hours. $500.

If you move forward with a project, the $500 comes off the cost.

Would this be useful for you or anyone you know? Happy to send the link.
```

## Template B — Church / Ministry Leader
*Best for: pastors, church admins, ministry directors you know*

```
Hey [Pastor/Name] —

I've been building an AI consultancy focused specifically on helping 
churches modernize without losing what makes them authentic.

I'm offering a discounted AI Readiness Call this month ($500) — 
60 minutes where we look at your current admin workflows, content 
production, and member communication, and I give you a written 
summary of where AI can genuinely help.

A lot of churches are wasting 10-15 hours a week on things that could 
run automatically. I'd love to show [Church Name] what that looks like.

Interested in a quick call first to see if it's a fit?
```

*Best for: business owners you don't know personally*

actually run.
