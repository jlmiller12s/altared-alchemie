Statement of Work / Phase One

Strategic Concept + Partner Prototype

A private, presentation-quality concept engagement for EMC Studios and America's Top Personal Trainer. This Phase One work is designed to make the ATPT vision tangible, premium, scalable, and commercially credible for confidential board, investor, and prospective partner conversations.

1. Engagement overview

Phase One is a private concept prototype only.

It does not include a public website, production software, source code, databases, or live integrations. All advanced functionality may be visually simulated.

Guiding assumptions

  • ATPT is the flagship property and receives the strongest visual emphasis.
  • TRAINTRAX will be demonstrated through a small number of high-impact concept screens.
  • Prospective sponsors will use neutral, replaceable placeholders unless Client Legal authorizes specific brand usage.
  • The prototype is intended to advance conversations, not represent an operational platform.

2. Scope of services

Discovery and creative direction

  • One structured kickoff and discovery session, up to 90 minutes.
  • Review of Client-provided strategy, references, logos, and approved content.
  • Clarification of audiences, objectives, and the core ATPT story.
  • One premium visual direction and priority screen list.

Prototype architecture

  • Simplified prototype sitemap or experience map.
  • One primary board and partner presentation journey.
  • Screen sequence and interaction planning.
  • Separation of ATPT, TRAINTRAX, EMC, and future properties.

High-fidelity concept design

  • Approximately 8 to 10 polished, desktop-first concept screens.
  • Up to three representative mobile compositions.
  • Two TRAINTRAX hero concepts.
  • One sponsor-neutral activation concept.

Clickable private prototype

  • Connected presentation flow in a mutually agreed prototyping tool.
  • Simulated navigation and selected interaction states.
  • Private, access-controlled sharing subject to platform capabilities.
  • Sample or placeholder content where final materials are unavailable.

Handoff and roadmap

  • Organized design and prototype source file after full payment.
  • Prototype presentation link or exported review file.
  • List of simulations and future production requirements.
  • One final presentation-readiness session, up to 60 minutes.

Screen-count rule

A screen is one distinct desktop composition. Alternate states, overlays, long-page sections, and mobile adaptations are not separate full-screen commitments unless expressly added in writing.

The fixed fee includes two structured revision checkpoints:

  2. One consolidated review of the assembled prototype before finalization.

Additional screens, user journeys, visual directions, revision cycles, or material restructuring require a written change order. If authorized without a fixed change-order fee, additional work will be billed at $100 per hour in 30-minute increments.
